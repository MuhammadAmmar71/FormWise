# Disclaimer

- **Unofficial.** This is an independent student portfolio project. It is not operated, endorsed or reviewed by any German authority, by the State of Berlin, or by Universität Koblenz.
- **Not legal advice.** Answers are generated by a language model from a small set of official texts and can still be wrong or incomplete. The binding rules are the law and the instructions of your local registration office (Bürgeramt / Meldebehörde).
- **No personal data.** Do not enter real personal data. The service has no accounts and does not store chat messages. Form-practice entries stay in your browser. Messages are sent to third-party LLM and embedding APIs (Groq and Google Gemini) to generate answers, after obvious personal data has been redacted.
- **Sources.** Statute texts are from gesetze-im-internet.de (official works, § 5 UrhG). Excerpts from service.berlin.de are used for explanation with attribution and links; all rights remain with the publisher. If you are a rights holder and want content removed, please open an issue.
